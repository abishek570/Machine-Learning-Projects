Heart Disease Prediction System

AI-Assisted Clinical Decision Support

Early Detection Saves Lives

Cardiovascular diseases remain one of the leading causes of mortality worldwide. Early identification of potential heart conditions plays a crucial role in preventing severe complications and improving patient outcomes.

This application uses a machine learning–based predictive model trained on clinical parameters such as age, blood pressure, cholesterol levels, ECG results, heart rate, and other medically relevant indicators to assess the likelihood of heart disease.

  • ✔ Clinically inspired input parameters
  • ✔ Structured and validated medical ranges
  • ✔ Real-time prediction support
  • ✔ Designed for educational and research use

How the Prediction Works

The system analyzes multiple patient health indicators and applies a trained classification model to estimate whether the patient is likely to have heart disease or not.

The prediction is based on patterns learned from historical medical datasets and is intended to assist clinicians and researchers in preliminary screening.
